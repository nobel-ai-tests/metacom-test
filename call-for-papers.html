Call for Papers

MetaCom invites original research on the systems, networks, interfaces, and applications that connect physical and digital environments.

Scope and Topics

MetaCom provides a forum for academic researchers and industry practitioners to present research progress, exchange new ideas, and identify future directions in computing, networking, and applications for the metaverse. Original contributions are solicited across the indicative areas listed below.

Computing and systems: architectures, edge and cloud computing, resource management
Networking: wireless systems, low-latency communication, traffic management
Digital twins: cyber-physical systems, modeling, synchronization, sensing
Interaction: extended reality, human-machine interfaces, haptics
Trust: security, privacy, safety, reliability, resilience
Intelligence: AI-enabled services, data analytics, intelligent environments
Applications: smart cities, industry, healthcare, finance, education, entertainment
Platforms and services: content, collaboration, interoperability, user experience

These areas summarize the scope of recent MetaCom editions and are provided for guidance. The official 2027 call will define the final topic list.
