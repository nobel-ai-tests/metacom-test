Submission Guidelines

Submission details for the MetaCom 2027 technical program will be posted here when the official portal opens.

2027 information to be announced

Paper Submission System

Submission portal: to be announced

The 2027 submission platform and opening date have not yet been announced. The official link will be published here together with the call for papers. Do not send manuscripts by email unless an official announcement specifically requests it.

Preparing Your Manuscript

The final call will specify the accepted paper categories, page limits, formatting template, file requirements, author metadata requirements, and any supplementary-material rules. These details are not being assumed from the 2026 edition or from other conferences.

  • Use the official 2027 template once it is published.
  • Check that the paper title, author details, and manuscript metadata are consistent.
  • Review the final policies on originality, prior publication, and simultaneous submission.
  • Follow the stated instructions for figures, references, supplementary files, and anonymization, if applicable.

Review and Decisions

The review model, review criteria, rebuttal process (if any), and notification dates will be announced in the 2027 call. Authors should use the official schedule for all deadlines.

Publication and Presentation

Proceedings publisher, indexing information, copyright process, and presentation requirements are pending confirmation. Acceptance alone should not be interpreted as a guarantee of publication until the official policy is released.
