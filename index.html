THE CONFERENCE

Research for a world
that is always connected.

MetaCom brings together academic researchers, innovators, and industry practitioners to explore the convergence of computing, networking, and applications for the metaverse.

As physical environments and digital systems become more closely linked, new possibilities emerge - and so do important research challenges. MetaCom is a place to share results, exchange perspectives, and help define what comes next.

◎

Connect worlds

Bring physical spaces, digital twins, people, and systems into closer conversation.

⌁

Advance networks

Explore the computing and communication foundations for responsive experiences.

✳

Enable impact

Translate new ideas into useful applications across society and industry.
