About the Conference

MetaCom provides a forum for academic researchers and industry practitioners to present research progress, exchange new ideas, and identify future directions in computing, networking, and applications for the metaverse. Topics span architectures and edge systems, wireless and low-latency networking, digital twins, extended reality and human interfaces, security and trust, and domain applications from smart cities to healthcare.

MetaCom 2027 will take place in New York City. See the Call for Papers for scope and topics.
