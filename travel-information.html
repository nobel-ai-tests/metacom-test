Travel Information

Getting to the conference venue in downtown Manhattan.

Public Transportation

The venue is one block west of Union Square. The museum's directions page lists these nearby stations:

  • 14 St – Union Sq: 4, 5, 6, L, N, Q, R, W trains
  • 14 St & 6 Av: F, M, L trains, and PATH
  • 14 St & 7 Av: 1, 2, 3 trains

Check the MTA website for service changes and trip planning. Transit guidance is also on the museum directions page.

Airports

New York City is served by John F. Kennedy International, LaGuardia, and Newark Liberty International airports. Travel time to Union Square varies with traffic. Rail links connect JFK and Newark via AirTrain to the subway system; LaGuardia is reached by bus connections.

Hotels

A conference hotel block has not yet been arranged. Hotels around Union Square and the Flatiron district will be the most convenient for the venue; the room block will be announced with the registration portal if one is secured.

Visiting the United States

International attendees should review current entry and visa requirements through official U.S. government sources. Letter-of-invitation procedures will be announced with registration.
