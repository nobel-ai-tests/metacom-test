Travel Information

Getting to Yeshiva University Museum in New York City.

Public Transportation

The museum is one block west of Union Square. The museum's official directions list these nearby subway stations:

  • 14th Street / Union Square: 4, 5, 6, L, N, Q, R
  • 14th Street / Sixth Avenue: F, M, L, PATH
  • 14th Street / Seventh Avenue: 1, 2, 3

Check the MTA website for current service and travel updates.

Airports and Accommodation

New York City is served by JFK, LaGuardia, and Newark Liberty airports. Ground travel times vary with traffic and time of day. No official MetaCom 2027 hotel block or preferred rate has been announced.

Visitor Resources

For the venue address and museum access information, see Conference Venue. International visitors should consult official U.S. government sources for current entry and visa requirements.
